## Projects

### 1. Superstore Sales Analysis & Executive Dashboard
**Tools:** Python, Power BI, DAX

This project focuses on analysing Superstore sales data to identify business performance trends and generate actionable insights.

Key areas include:
- Sales and profit performance.
- Regional sales comparisons.
- Product category and sub-category analysis.
- Monthly sales trends.
- Product profitability.
- Interactive executive dashboard with KPI indicators.

### 2. Telecom Customer Churn Analysis
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

This project analyses customer churn behaviour using the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset.

Key areas include:
- Customer retention and churn rates.
- Contract type analysis.
- Payment method analysis.
- Customer tenure and monthly charges.
- Identification of customer segments associated with higher churn.
